What is Green Technology?

Green technology, or "clean tech," is an umbrella term for technologies that are environmentally friendly and are created to combat the negative impacts of human activity on the planet. This can include renewable energy sources like solar and wind power, sustainable transportation methods, and innovative waste management systems.

The goal of green technology is to reduce our carbon footprint, conserve natural resources, and create a more sustainable way of life for generations to come. By adopting and promoting these technologies, we can build a future that is both prosperous and in harmony with our environment.
