E-ASHA: A Digital Healthcare Support System for ASHA Workers

E-ASHA is a digital healthcare support system designed to simplify and modernize the daily work of Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHA) in rural and semi-urban communities. ASHA workers play a crucial role in India’s public health system, yet they often face challenges such as managing patient data manually, limited access to medical information, difficulty in tracking patient history, and lack of real-time decision support. To address these challenges, E-ASHA integrates technology with community healthcare to create a smart, user-friendly, and efficient platform.

Remote-Control River Cleaning

To overcome these challenges, a Remote-Control River Cleaning Boat has been developed.
